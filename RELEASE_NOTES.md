Minutes 2.10.0 adds inline AI editing and a redesigned meeting assistant.

- Open the assistant from the floating button at the bottom right, or press Command-J. The panel has a cleaner header, suggested questions, conversation bubbles and a focused composer.
- Press Space on a blank line in the notes editor to open inline AI. Selecting text with the mouse opens contextual tools; Ask AI in the formatting toolbar also works with keyboard selections.
- Make a selection concise, improve its writing, explain it, or enter a custom instruction. On a blank line, continue your notes or extract key points from captured meeting context.
- AI results stream into a preview. Replace the selected passage, insert below, or discard. If your document changes before applying, Minutes preserves it and asks you to select again. Applied edits support Undo.
- The same rich editor and AI tools are available in Edit AI notes. Read-only generated notes remain selectable for copying; click Edit to use inline tools there.
- Uses the AI provider selected in Settings. Cloud editing sends the selected text, instruction and selected meeting context to that provider. Apple editing stays on the Mac and avoids overlapping local generation jobs.
- Active inline work blocks updater installation. Closing a preview cancels its job; it does not alter your saved notes.

The Notion-inspired editing interactions work within Minutes; this release does not reproduce Notion databases, block collaboration or all Notion features.
