Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Minutes is an open-source Mac app maintained by Max Litwack (GitHub: ashclient). This page explains what the app stores and what optional connections share.

What stays on your Mac

Minutes stores recordings, transcripts, typed notes, generated notes, meeting metadata and preferences on your Mac. Saved recordings are not uploaded to NVIDIA, OpenRouter or Notion by the app. Google, Notion and AI-provider credentials and user tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. You can create exports or backups yourself; those files are then under your control.

Recording and Apple features

Recording starts when you choose it and grant the required macOS permissions. Microphone capture needs microphone access; computer audio uses ScreenCaptureKit and may request screen/system-audio capture permission. Minutes saves audio, not screen video. Apple transcription and the optional Apple Intelligence feature use the supported system features on your Mac. Apple software and services remain subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Optional cloud AI

If you choose NVIDIA or OpenRouter, Minutes sends the selected meeting's transcript, typed notes and relevant title/context to that provider to generate or review notes. Audio files are not sent. OpenRouter may route the text to the provider serving the model you select. Provider account terms, retention rules and usage limits apply. Apple Intelligence does not automatically fall back to a cloud AI provider.

Google Calendar access

Connecting Google requests read-only access to your calendar list and events. Minutes uses calendar names, event titles, times and related event metadata to display meetings, open the right note and name linked notes. It does not request permission to create, edit or delete Google Calendar events.

When a hosted sign-in service is configured, authorization codes, PKCE proofs and access/refresh tokens pass through that service to exchange or refresh access with Google. The service keeps the application's registration secret server-side. The included service code does not persist tokens or log request bodies. User tokens remain in your Mac's Keychain between requests. Calendar event bodies are fetched directly from Google by the Mac app. Existing custom Desktop connections may exchange directly with Google instead.

Calendar titles and dates attached to a note may be included in the AI or Notion content you choose to send. Minutes does not send your whole calendar to an AI provider. You can disconnect in Minutes or revoke access through Google account connections. Previously saved notes and event metadata are not erased by disconnecting.

Minutes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by the Minutes project to train a general-purpose AI model.

Notion sync

If you connect Notion and enable sync, Minutes sends the selected note's content and relevant meeting title/date to the Notion destination you choose. Depending on your sync options, that content can include typed notes, generated notes and transcript text. Notion access is used to find the pages you allow, create or update meeting pages, and check for edits. A Notion copy remains in Notion until you delete it there. Notion's privacy policy applies to its service.

Updates, website and service hosting

Update checks contact GitHub for release metadata and downloads. GitHub receives ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request metadata. This GitHub Pages website includes no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies added by the Minutes project. GitHub processes hosting traffic under its own privacy statement. If a hosted token service is deployed, its hosting provider also processes the requests needed to run that service; the supplied deployment targets Cloudflare.

GitHub privacy · Cloudflare privacy

Retention and deletion

The Minutes project does not operate a central database of your recordings or notes. Local files remain until you remove them. Moving a note to Trash is recoverable; permanently deleting it removes its associated local files through the app. Removing the app alone may leave its Application Support data and Keychain items. Backups, exported files, Notion copies and content retained by a selected AI provider require separate deletion with those services. Disconnecting a connection stops its future use but does not delete previously exported content.

Sharing and access

The project does not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising. Data goes to the optional services you enable for the functions described above. Avoid posting recordings, private notes, credentials or calendar details in public bug reports. If you request support, share only the minimum information needed and remove sensitive content first.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or a private reporting channel, start with the contact options on the Support page. Do not put secrets or personal meeting content into a public issue.
