Use Google Calendar to find a meeting and optionally sync its notes to a new or existing Notion page.

A short path from
recording to notes.

  1. Open a calendar event or start a blank note.
  2. Record and add your own thoughts.
  3. Finish, choose the detail level, and review the result.
  4. Keep it in Minutes, or send it to Notion.

Choose where AI runs.

Use Apple Intelligence on an eligible Mac, or connect your own NVIDIA or OpenRouter API account. Cloud AI receives the text needed to write your notes; saved audio stays on your Mac.

Apple Intelligence currently requires macOS 27 and a ready model. Other Apple speech features depend on your Mac, language, and macOS version. Google and Notion connections are optional.
